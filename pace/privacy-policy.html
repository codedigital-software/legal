Privacy Policy

Pace watch faces (including Pace – Sport Analog and Pace Words) · Last updated: 21 September 2026

Summary

Pace watch faces do not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything they show is read and displayed on your watch only.

Data shown on the watch face

Depending on the design, a Pace watch face can display the time and date, battery level, weather, step count and heart rate. This information comes from your watch's own system and sensors. It is used only to draw the watch face and never leaves your device.

Permissions

  • Body sensors (heart rate): used only to show your current heart rate on the watch face.
  • Physical activity (steps): used only to show today's step count on the watch face.

You can deny or revoke these permissions at any time in your watch settings. The watch face keeps working without them. The related values simply won't be shown.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or tracking.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No data is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Children

The watch faces don't collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
